# Movement prototype

Run the project with F6 on `src/scenes/main_scene.tscn`, or F5.

- WASD: move; Shift while moving forward: sprint.
- Hold Ctrl or C: crouch. Release to stand when there is overhead clearance.
- Mouse: look; Escape: release cursor; click: capture again.
- F3: toggle subtle head bob; R: return to spawn.
- No manual jump: test the stairs and walk off the landing for a small drop.

The reusable `src/scenes/player.tscn` uses `src/scripts/player.gd`.
Movement, collision dimensions, mouse sensitivity, and camera feedback are exported in the Inspector.
The player's origin is at its feet; keep its scale at (1, 1, 1).
The test room is generated by `src/scripts/movement_test_room.gd`.
Its visible stairs use an invisible collision ramp. True step-up traversal is a future extension.

Try stopping after sprinting, strafing against boxes, entering the low passage,
releasing crouch under its roof, and looking up while walking forward.
The prototype has no weapon animations, stamina, controller look, or interaction system yet.
